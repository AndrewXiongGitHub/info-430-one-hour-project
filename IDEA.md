# My app

**Domain: FC Barcelona La Masia Academy Talent Tracker**

**User story:** As a supporter of FC Barcelona, I need to know more about up-and-coming academy prospects from La Masia, so that I can be more informed on the players to watch.

**What the user types or picks:** A text search for a prospect's name, position, or nationality; an optional academy squad; and optional minimum and maximum ages from 14 to 20. Leave a filter blank to skip it. Results include the player's age, academy squad, strengths, development focus, and a scouting note about what to watch for.
